# TimelineItem

One role in the Experience timeline: dates on the left, a rail dot, role @ company, 2–3 bullets and tags.

- Provide `role`, `company`, `period` ("Mar 2025 – Present"), `bullets` (what you built/achieved, plain language, start with a verb), `tags`, `current` (fills the dot in accent).
- Wrap items in `<ol class="am-timeline">`, newest first.
- Under 720px the date moves above the role.
